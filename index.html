Capstone: Kivo Health AI Documentation Assistant

In-progress senior capstone focused on AI-assisted healthcare documentation, workflow automation, and clinician-centered product design.

In Progress • Spring 2026

Healthcare AI • OCR • Product Design • Human-in-the-Loop Review

Reducing administrative burden for respiratory therapists

Kivo Health respiratory therapists work with faxed and scanned medical documents that contain patient history, assessment details, and treatment information. These documents can be time-consuming to review manually, especially when therapists need to create Initial Treatment Plans for multiple patients. Our capstone project explores how OCR, structured extraction, and AI-assisted drafting can support clinicians while keeping therapists in full control of final documentation.

Project Goal

The goal is to build an AI-assisted workflow that reduces the time respiratory therapists spend drafting Initial Treatment Plans while preserving clinical judgment, transparency, and human review.

  • Extract key patient information from scanned or faxed documents.
  • Organize information into a structured intake workflow.
  • Generate draft Initial Treatment Plans for clinician review.
  • Keep the therapist as the final decision-maker before anything is finalized.

Team Process

As a team, we moved from problem discovery to MVP planning, prototyping, feedback, and iteration. We used our winter MVP presentation and spring project plan to refine what the product should do and what should stay out of scope.

  • Reviewed the existing documentation workflow and pain points.
  • Identified where manual work slows down respiratory therapists.
  • Created MVP screens to show the intake and treatment-plan flow.
  • Discussed ethical risks around automation bias and overreliance on AI.
  • Adjusted the product direction based on feedback and feasibility constraints.

My Individual Contributions

My role has focused on connecting product thinking, user workflow, and technical execution. I worked on making the project easier to explain, evaluate, and demo.

  • Helped define product requirements and the core user workflow.
  • Built React/Vite prototype screens for patient intake and draft review.
  • Translated clinician pain points into product features and MVP priorities.
  • Helped shape the evaluation plan around time savings, accuracy, and usability.
  • Focused on making the final system feel supportive rather than replacing clinicians.

Skills I Built

This capstone helped me grow across product management, healthcare workflow analysis, technical prototyping, and responsible AI design.

  • Product requirement writing and MVP scoping.
  • Healthcare workflow analysis and stakeholder-centered design.
  • React/Vite prototyping for realistic user flows.
  • AI product evaluation, including accuracy and trust considerations.
  • Communicating technical ideas to non-technical stakeholders.

Current Takeaways

The biggest lesson so far is that the hard part is not only generating content with AI. The real challenge is designing a workflow that clinicians can trust. A useful healthcare AI system needs to show where information came from, make uncertainty visible, and make review easy instead of hiding important decisions behind automation.
